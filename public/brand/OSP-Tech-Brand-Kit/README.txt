OSP TECH — BRAND KIT
====================
Full guidance: 05-Brand-Guidelines/OSP-Tech-Brand-Guidelines.pdf

QUICK GUIDE: WHICH LOGO WHERE
  Profile pictures (Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn) -> 02-Social-Media/profile-picture-*.png  (icon only)
  Website header ............ 01-Logos/2-Horizontal-No-Tagline (SVG). Phones: 6-Icon-Only
  Website footer ............ 01-Logos/1-Primary-Horizontal-with-Tagline (on-dark)
  Favicon ................... 01-Logos/7-Favicon-and-App-Icons (copy all files to the website root)
  Covers & banners .......... 02-Social-Media/*cover*, *banner*, *header*
  Instagram posts ........... small 2-Horizontal-No-Tagline in a corner; 3-Stacked on the last slide
  Watermark on client work .. 02-Social-Media/watermarks (set 15-25% opacity)
  Business card ............. 03-Print/Business-Card (send the PRINT pdf to the printer)
  Letters, quotes, invoices . 03-Print/Letterhead (Word template or PDF)
  Email signature ........... 04-Email-Signature
  Stamps / one-colour print . any logo in the "-navy" colourway

COLOURWAYS (in every logo folder)
  -color    light backgrounds        -on-dark  navy / dark backgrounds
  -white    blue or photo backgrounds -navy     one-colour printing

FILE TYPES
  SVG = master, sharp at any size (website, print shop, Illustrator, Figma, Canva Pro)
  PNG = transparent, 3000px (Canva, Word, PowerPoint, social apps)
  PDF = print-ready (business card has 3mm bleed; fonts converted to shapes)

COLOURS   Blue #3871FC   Teal #04BCC8   Navy #081B33   Blue Strong #2457D6   Cloud #F3F6FB   Slate #5B6576
FONT      Poppins (free, Google Fonts) — ExtraBold Italic for headlines, Regular for text

BEFORE PRINTING: the phone number, email and website on the business card, letterhead
and email signature are PLACEHOLDERS. Replace them with your real details first.
